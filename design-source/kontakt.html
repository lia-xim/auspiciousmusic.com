Contact

Corrections, rights questions and editorial enquiries all reach the same desk. Corrections are acted on and recorded on the page they concern.

What to write about

  • Corrections. A wrong figure, a mis-stated version, a broken step, a bad attribution. Include the page and what you observed.
  • Rights and archive. If you hold rights in anything published on this domain before 2026, or believe a historical page misrepresents your work, this is the fastest route.
  • Editorial. Suggestions for guides, gaps you keep hitting, or a technique you would like tested.
  • Press. Enquiries about the publication itself and its relaunch.

The form below is designed but not yet connected. It goes live together with the operator details required for the Impressum, so that a message has a named, reachable recipient rather than disappearing into an unattended mailbox.

How corrections are handled

A substantive correction is made on the page and recorded there with its date, rather than quietly overwritten. Historical and attribution errors are treated as urgent. The full standard is set out in the editorial policy.
