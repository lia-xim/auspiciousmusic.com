Editorial policy

How pages here are researched, tested, sourced and corrected — written down so it can be held against us.

What every page must contain

A page is not published unless it delivers at least one of the following: a workflow you can reproduce, a test you can follow, original screenshots or audio, a specific problem solved, a properly researched historical account, or a resource list that has been checked and dated.

Nothing here exists to occupy a search term. If a page cannot meet that bar, it does not get written.

Testing and versions

  • Version and date are statedTechnical articles name the software version, operating system and test date. A guide with no date is a guide with no value.
  • Untested is labelled untestedWhere something was researched rather than run, the page says so plainly instead of implying first-hand experience.
  • Failures stay inWhere a method broke, the article documents the break and the fix. Removing them makes for a cleaner read and a less useful one.
  • Screenshots are oursInterface images come from the machine the article was written on, at the stated version.

Sources

Factual claims are sourced, preferring primary documentation over secondary coverage. Where a figure comes from a manufacturer or a platform, it is dated, because those figures change. Where something is convention rather than fact — a starting point for a reverb decay, for instance — it is described as such.

AI assistance

Language tools are used in production here, for drafting structure, editing and checking. They are not used to generate technical claims, and no page is published on the strength of a model's assertion about how a piece of software behaves. Anything technical is verified against the software itself or against primary documentation.

No page is published as generated text. Every article passes through a human who is answerable for its claims. If a claim here is wrong, it is a person's error, not a tool's.

Commercial relationships

  • Disclosure is on the pageAny commercial relationship relevant to an article is stated in the article, not buried in a policy page.
  • One commercial link at mostAn article carries no more than one primary commercial link, and only where it genuinely serves the reader.
  • No article exists for a linkIf removing the link would leave nothing worth publishing, the page should not exist.
  • No paid placement in reviewsReview verdicts are not for sale, and equipment received for testing would be disclosed as such.

Corrections

Errors are corrected on the page, with the change recorded and dated rather than quietly overwritten. Substantial corrections are noted at the point of the correction. Historical and attribution errors are treated as urgent.

What this publication will not do

  • Invent authors, reviews or testimonialsEvery name on this site belongs to a real person.
  • Invent metricsNo unsourced performance claims, no fabricated popularity figures, no star ratings without a stated method.
  • Redistribute work it has no right toIncluding anything published by the former operator of this domain.
  • Backdate a publication dateHistorical context is shown as historical context, never as a current publication date.

Auspicious Music was relaunched under new ownership in 2026. Historical references are preserved for context.
