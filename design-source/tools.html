Tools

Small calculators for the arithmetic that comes up mid-session. Each one runs entirely in your browser, needs no account, and works the same on a phone in a live room as on a desktop.

Available tools

  • Delay & Reverb Time

    Every note division in milliseconds and hertz for a given tempo — straight, dotted and triplet — with reverb pre-delay and decay starting points. Includes a tap-tempo control for when you have the track but not the number.

    BPM → ms · HzTap tempoCopy on click

    Open

  • Note & Frequency

    Note names, MIDI numbers and frequencies in both directions across the full instrumental range. Set the concert pitch, enter a frequency, and read the cents deviation to the nearest note — plus the wavelength, for room problems.

    Note ⇄ Hz ⇄ MIDI415–443 HzCents · wavelength

    Open

  • Sample Pitch & Rate

    What transposing a sample does to its playback rate, its length and its tempo when nothing is time-stretched. Also works out sample-rate mismatch in semitones, which is usually the answer when an import sounds slightly sharp.

    Semitones → rateLength · tempo44.1 / 48 kHz

    Open

How these work

Nothing leaves your browser
Every calculation runs locally. No values are transmitted, logged or stored, and there is nothing to sign in to.
The arithmetic is written out
Each tool states the formula it uses, so you can check it rather than trust it. Where a figure is a convention rather than a fact, the page says so.
Usable on a phone in a live room
Large controls, tabular figures, and every result one tap from the clipboard. Full keyboard operation on desktop.
No advertising inside a tool
A calculator you reach for mid-session should not be a surface for anything else. Related reading sits below the tool, never in front of it.
