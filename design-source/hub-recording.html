Terms used across this hub

Proximity effect
The bass lift that appears as a directional microphone moves closer to a source — a tone control, once you know it is there.
Spot microphone
A close microphone on one instrument within a wider setup, mixed under the main pair for definition.
Room mode
A standing wave at a frequency whose wavelength fits a dimension of the room, making some notes far louder in some positions.
Gain staging
Setting level at every stage so nothing clips and nothing sits so low that noise becomes audible later.
