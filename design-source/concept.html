Design concept · revision 2 · for approval

A measuring instrument, published as a magazine

This page is the reviewer's index. It is not part of the site — the publication itself starts at index.html, which is where the wordmark leads from every page.

The site opens on graphite at full bleed, then runs on paper neutral. One spectral line threads the opening sections; the archive and the lab are the only places material returns. Indigo means action, ember means archive, and nothing uses both.

What changed in revision 2

Four things, in response to the first review.

  • The hero — now at revision 4Revisions 2 and 3 both put the argument in a photograph — first a full-bleed 21:9 band that cropped the object into an anonymous strip, then a portrait frame beside the type. Revision 4 drops photography from the masthead entirely: a full-viewport scene carried by two lines of block type, with one taut string across the headline that answers the pointer and rings down on real wave physics. The object photograph moved to the lab band, where it belongs.
  • Full-viewport mastheadThe hero is now calc(100svh − header), which deliberately reverses the earlier rule about keeping the next section in view. The Latest row sits as the closing line inside the viewport so the scene still reads as continuing rather than ending.
  • German legal layerImpressum (§ 5 DDG, § 18 Abs. 2 MStV), Datenschutzerklärung (Art. 13 DSGVO, § 25 TDDDG) and an accessibility statement, in German with English summaries. Operator fields are marked as required rather than invented.
  • Touch layerA final cascade layer that fixes what a desktop-first stylesheet gets wrong on a phone: 16px form fields so iOS stops zooming on focus, safe-area insets for notched devices in landscape, hover tints moved to :active on hover-less pointers so rows do not stay highlighted after a tap, and 44px targets in the dense footer and breadcrumb stacks.
  • The paletteThe canvas moved from #f5f5f7 to a paper neutral, and a second accent axis was added with a strict split of meaning — indigo for action, ember for archive and material. That is what removes the clinical feel; a single accent on a bright neutral had nothing to push against.
  • The entry pointindex.html is now the homepage, so the wordmark behaves the way a wordmark must. This concept index moved to concept.html.
  • DepthEvery hub in the navigation now exists, the trust pages are written, and a tools section has been added with three working calculators.

Motion

  • Hero lines — mask reveal, 620 ms, 80 ms stagger
  • Hero image — settles from scale 1.04, 1400 ms, opacity never below 1
  • Spectral rail — ember node tracks scroll through the first bands
  • Index rows — 16 px rise, 60 ms stagger, capped at six
  • Timelines — same reveal, per entry
  • Header — border and surface resolve past 64 px

No pinning, no scroll-jacking, no smooth-scroll library, no loops. Elements are only hidden if they start below the fold, and a 2.5-second failsafe releases anything the observer misses — so content never depends on a callback arriving.

Open decisions

Unchanged from revision 1, plus one new item.

  • Placeholder mediaScreenshot and photograph slots are labelled grey placeholders stating what to capture and at what size. The three photographs that exist were generated for this concept and should be replaced with real studio shots.
  • Version and date values“Ableton Live 12” and the 2026 dates are realistic placeholders. Each must be replaced with an actual tested version and test date before publication.
  • BylineArticles are credited to the editorial desk because the spec bars invented authors. Real author records are needed before launch.
  • SearchThe header control and the 404 field are designed but inert. Pagefind is the intended backing once enough content exists.
  • Inter licence and hostingLoaded from Google Fonts in the concept; self-host as a variable subset for production.
  • Tools and the navigationTools sit under Resources, because the brief fixes the seven navigation items. If the section performs, it may deserve its own position — that is a decision to take on data, not now.
  • Hub topic countsThe hubs carried a “7 guides” column in the first generated pass. It asserted volume that does not exist, so it was removed. The column returns in Astro as a figure derived from the content collection, where it will be true by construction.
