About Auspicious Music

An independent publication for music production, sound design, recording and creative audio technology — relaunched under new ownership in 2026.

What this publication does

Auspicious Music writes about making music with computers and with instruments, and refuses to treat those as separate subjects. Ableton Live and Max for Live, sampling and synthesis, recording acoustic instruments and strings, arrangement, and writing music for film, video and occasions.

Every technical page states the software version it was tested against and the date of that test. Where something was researched rather than tested, the page says so in those words. That standard is set out in full in the editorial policy.

Who it is for

  • Producers and electronic musiciansPeople working in a DAW who want a method rather than a preset.
  • Ableton and Max for Live usersFrom a first session through to building devices that solve a specific problem.
  • Composers for film, video and mediaWriting to a brief, to a picture, and to a delivery specification.
  • Instrumentalists moving into productionPlayers integrating an acoustic instrument into a digital workflow.
  • Small studios and creatorsAnyone with a practical recording or workflow problem and no technical department to ask.

Authors

Articles are currently credited to the editorial desk while the author records are being set up. This publication does not invent bylines: no article will carry a name that does not belong to a real person who wrote or reviewed it, and author pages will link to verifiable work.

Relationship to other projects

The publisher works on other things, including music and video projects. Where one of those is genuinely relevant to an article — a recording used as an example, a real project used as a case study — it may be linked, and the relationship is disclosed on the page. There are no such links in the navigation or the global footer, and no article exists in order to carry one.

Contact and corrections

Corrections are welcome and are acted on. If a technical claim, a date, an attribution or a historical detail is wrong, it gets fixed and the change is recorded on the page. That applies with particular force to anything concerning the former operator of this domain.

Contact details are not yet published. They go live with the operator information required for the legal notice, rather than being put up as a placeholder address that nobody reads.

The domain's history

This address hosted a different music-technology site under a different operator between roughly 2013 and 2017, and was later exploited for spam while it lay dormant. That history is documented rather than hidden — see history and relaunch for the full account, and the archive for the status of every historic resource.

Auspicious Music was relaunched under new ownership in 2026. Historical references are preserved for context.
