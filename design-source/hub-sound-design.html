Sound design and sampling

Making a sound rather than choosing one. Sampling, synthesis and instrument building, each written up with the settings that produced the result and the points where it stopped behaving. Where audio is relevant, it is recorded for the guide rather than borrowed.

Terms used across this hub

Round-robin
Cycling through several recordings of the same note so repeated notes do not sound identical.
Velocity layer
A separate recording used across a range of playing strengths, so dynamics change timbre and not just volume.
Zero crossing
The point where a waveform passes through silence — cutting there avoids the click a mid-waveform edit produces.
Formant
The resonance that gives a source its character. Transposing a sample too far moves it, which is why a repitched voice sounds artificial.
