History and relaunch

What Auspicious Music is today, what was published at this address before 2026, and exactly where the line between the two runs.

Auspicious Music was relaunched under new ownership in 2026 as an independent publication. Historical references are preserved for context.

The current publication does not represent the former operator and makes no claim to their work, clients, credits, releases or partnerships. Nothing here should be read as endorsed by them unless it is explicitly stated with their permission.

The publication today

Auspicious Music publishes practical guides for music production, Ableton Live and Max for Live, sound design and sampling, recording acoustic instruments and strings, and composing and delivering music for film, video and events.

Every technical page states the software version it was tested against and the date of that test. Where something was not tested, the page says so. The editorial policy sets out how that works, including the use of AI assistance, how corrections are handled, and how any commercial relationship is disclosed.

What was here before

Between roughly 2013 and 2017 this domain hosted a music-technology site run by a different operator, covering Ableton Live and Push, Max for Live devices, sampled instruments, controller mappings and production workflows. Several of those resources — The Spy Guitar, Bang My Twister and others — were widely referenced and are still linked from external pages today.

After that period the site stopped being maintained, and the domain was subsequently used to host injected spam pages that had nothing to do with music. That is a common fate for a lapsed domain with real links pointing at it.

How the relaunch handles all of that

Three rules, applied without exception:

Historic addresses that carried real intent stay answerable. Where someone following a decade-old link was looking for a specific resource, they reach a page about that resource — documented as history, with its rights position stated and a current alternative offered where one exists. They are not silently redirected to the homepage.

Nothing is reissued without the right to reissue it. The former operator's files, text, audio and images did not transfer with the domain. None of it is republished here. The downloads page lists every historic file with its actual status rather than a broken button.

Injected spam paths are removed, not redirected. Confirmed hacked and gambling-spam addresses return a permanent removal response, and unknown addresses return an ordinary not-found page. There is no catch-all redirect, because a catch-all would pass that history straight into the new publication.

Timeline

  • 2013 – 2017

    Previous site active

    Music-technology publishing under the former operator: Ableton coverage, Max for Live devices, sampled instruments and controller mappings.

  • After 2017

    Site no longer maintained

    Publishing stops. Over the following years the address is exploited for injected spam pages unrelated to music.

  • February 2026

    Domain acquired by the current publisher

    Historic addresses audited against archive records and backlink data. Spam paths identified and marked for permanent removal.

  • August 2026

    Independent relaunch

    Auspicious Music relaunches as an independent publication with new editorial work and a documented archive layer.

Corrections and contact

If something on this site is wrong — a technical claim, a date, a historical detail, or an attribution — write to us and it gets corrected with the change recorded on the page. That applies with particular force to anything concerning the former operator's work.
