What the original project was

The Spy Guitar was a sampled electric guitar instrument for Ableton Live, built around a heavily processed clean tone and distributed as a free Live Pack from this domain. It was released alongside a visualiser project and promoted through the Ableton and controller communities of the period.

It belongs to a specific moment: the years when Max for Live and free community Live Packs were how producers discovered each other's work, before that traffic moved to marketplaces and video platforms.

Why external sources still point here

The instrument was covered by production blogs and referenced in controller mapping communities, and several of those pages are still online and still linking to this address. That is the reason this URL returns a page rather than a redirect: the incoming intent is specific, and answering it with the homepage would be a worse outcome than answering it honestly.

What is still verifiable today

The archived pages, the external coverage, and the announcement history are all documented and independently checkable — those are listed under sources below. What is not verifiable is the current licence position of the audio content and the instrument files themselves, which is why they are not reissued here in any form.
